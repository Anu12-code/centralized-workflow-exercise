# Pancake Recipe
## Preparation
- Preheat griddle or non-stick pan
- Lightly grease with butter or oil
- Prepare mixing bowl and whisk
- Use 1 cup of flour mix per batch (makes ~6 pancakes)

## Ingredients
- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- 2 tbsp sugar
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 0.5 tsp baking soda
- 0.25 tsp salt
- 1 egg
- 1 cup buttermilk (or milk + 1 tsp vinegar)
- 2 tbsp melted butter or oil
- Optional: vanilla extract, cinnamon, blueberries, chocolate chips

## Instructions
- Mix dry ingredients in a bowl
- In separate bowl, beat egg and combine with buttermilk and melted butter
- Pour wet mixture into dry ingredients
- Stir gently until just combined (lumps are okay)
- Let batter rest for 5–10 min

## Cooking
- Pour 1/4 cup batter per pancake onto hot griddle
- Cook until bubbles form and edges look set (~2 min)
- Flip and cook other side until golden (~1–2 min)
- Serve warm with syrup, fruit, or your favorite toppings
