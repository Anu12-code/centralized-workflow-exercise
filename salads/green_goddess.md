# Green Goddess salad recipe


## Ingredients

- 600g white cabbage
- 1 cucumber
- 2 scalions
- 20g chives
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1 shallot
- 30g basil
- 30g baby spinach
- 80 cashews
- 80ml olive oil
- 2 lemons
- 12g nutritional yeast
- 1 tsp salt


## Instructions

1. **Prepare:**
   - Finely chop white cabbage and cucumber. Thinly slice scallions. Put all in a large bowl

2. **Souce:**
   - Juice lemons
   - Add in a blender:
        - Garlic
        - shallot
        - chives
        - basil
        - spinach
        - cashews
        - nutritional yeast
        - olive oil
        - lemon juice
    - season with salt
    Blend until smooth, add more olive oil to loosen if needed.

53. **Serve:**
   - Enjoy the salad as is, or eat it with tortilla chips.
