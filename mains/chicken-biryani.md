# Chicken Biryani

## Ingredients

- 500g chicken, cut into pieces
- 2 cups basmati rice
- 1 large onion, thinly sliced
- 2 ripe tomatoes, chopped
- 1/2 cup plain yogurt
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1-inch piece ginger, grated
- 2 green chilies, slit
- 1/4 cup fresh coriander leaves, chopped
- 1/4 cup fresh mint leaves, chopped
- 3 tablespoons cooking oil or ghee
- 2 cups water (for cooking rice)
- Salt to taste

**Spices:**

- 1 bay leaf
- 2 green cardamom pods
- 2 cloves
- 1 cinnamon stick (1-inch)
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 1/2 tsp turmeric powder
- 1 tsp red chili powder
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1/2 tsp biryani masala (optional)

## Instructions

- Rinse the basmati rice in cold water until the water runs clear. Soak the rice in water for 30 minutes, then drain and set aside.
- Heat oil or ghee in a heavy-bottomed pot or pan. Add bay leaf, cardamom, cloves, cinnamon, and cumin seeds. Sauté for 30 seconds until aromatic.
- Add sliced onions and cook over medium heat until golden brown. Remove half the onions and set aside for garnish.
- Add ginger, garlic, and green chilies to the pot. Sauté for 1–2 minutes until fragrant.
- Stir in chopped tomatoes and cook until soft and the oil begins to separate.
- Add chicken pieces and cook for 5–7 minutes, stirring occasionally.
- Mix in yogurt, turmeric, red chili powder, garam masala, biryani masala (if using), salt, and half of the chopped coriander and mint. Cook for another 10 minutes, until the chicken is tender and the masala thickens.
- In a separate pot, bring 2 cups of water to a boil. Add the soaked and drained rice and cook for 5–6 minutes until partially cooked (rice should still have a slight bite). Drain immediately.
- Layer the half-cooked rice over the chicken mixture in the pot. Sprinkle the reserved fried onions, remaining herbs, and a pinch of saffron (optional, soaked in warm milk).
- Cover tightly with a lid or seal with foil to trap steam. Cook on low heat (dum style) for 20–25 minutes to allow flavors to meld.
- Gently fluff the biryani with a fork before serving. Serve hot with raita or salad.
