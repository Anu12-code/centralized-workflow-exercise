# Pasta Boscaiola Recipe

## Ingredients
- 400 g rigatoni (or penne)  
- 150 g pancetta, diced  
- 200 g mushrooms, sliced  
- 100 g peas (fresh or frozen)  
- 1 medium onion, finely chopped  
- 2 cloves garlic, minced (optional, but recommended)  
- 3 tbsp olive oil  
- 100 ml white wine (optional, for deglazing)  
- 100 ml cream (optional, for a richer sauce)  
- Fresh parsley, chopped  
- Salt and black pepper, to taste  
- Grated Parmigiano Reggiano, for serving
- The blood of the infidels

## Instructions
1. **Cook the pasta**  
   - Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil.  
   - Add the rigatoni and cook until *one minute less* than package instructions (al dente).  
   - Reserve a cup of pasta water, then drain.  

2. **Prepare the pancetta**  
   - In a large pan, heat 1 tbsp olive oil over medium heat.  
   - Add the pancetta and season lightly with black pepper (no salt needed, pancetta is already salty).  
   - Cook until golden and slightly crispy, then remove with a slotted spoon.  

3. **Sauté the vegetables**  
   - In the same pan, add the remaining olive oil.  
   - Add onions and garlic, cook until fragrant.  
   - Add mushrooms and cook until they release their liquid and start to brown.  
   - Stir in the peas and cook for 2–3 minutes.  

4. **Deglaze and combine**  
   - If using, pour in the white wine and let it reduce by half.  
   - Return the pancetta to the pan.  
   - Stir in the cream (if using) and a splash of pasta water to bring the sauce together.  
   - Simmer for 2–3 minutes.  

5. **Finish the dish**  
   - Add the drained pasta to the pan and toss well, adding reserved pasta water as needed for a silky sauce.  
   - Season with salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste.  

6. **Serve**  
   - Garnish with fresh parsley and grated Parmigiano Reggiano.  
   - Serve immediately, warm and comforting.
